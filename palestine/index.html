Verified Donation Channels

Please ensure you are donating through official platforms. Below are trusted international and local organizations actively operating on the ground.

  • UNRWA (United Nations Relief and Works Agency) The official UN agency providing direct food, healthcare, and education to Palestine refugees. 100% of Zakat funds go directly to those eligible.
  • PCRF (Palestine Children's Relief Fund) A non-profit organization providing free medical care and humanitarian aid to children in the Middle East, regardless of nationality or religion.
  • BAZNAS RI (National Zakat Agency of Indonesia) Indonesia's official government agency for transparent distribution of Zakat, Infaq, and Sadaqah for Palestine.
  • Palestine Red Crescent Society (PRCS) An independent humanitarian organization providing emergency medical services and ambulance support directly inside Palestine.

Important Notes Before Donating

⚠️ Please Read Carefully

  • Beware of Scams: Only use official, verified websites. Do not send money to unknown personal accounts on social media.
  • Transparency: Legitimate organizations (like UNRWA and BAZNAS) provide public financial reports detailing how funds are distributed.
  • Zakat Compliance: If you are paying Zakat, ensure the organization explicitly states that 100% of the funds go directly to the beneficiaries without administrative deductions.
  • Stay Informed: Rely on credible news sources (UN OCHA, major international news agencies) and avoid spreading unverified information or hoaxes.
